Opened in2015

From a two-bay garage to a full-service workshop

Torque & Tread opened in 2015 with two lifts, one compressor, and a founder who couldn't stand watching commuters get overcharged for basic servicing.

Word spread through a college campus, then a delivery fleet, then sport-bike riders who'd been turned away by workshops without the tools for fuel-injected engines. A decade later the principle is the same: diagnose properly, use the right parts, and explain the invoice in plain language.
