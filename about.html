Our Story

From a Two-Bay Garage to a Full-Service Workshop

Torque & Tread opened its doors in 2015 with two lifts, one compressor, and a founder who couldn't stand watching commuters get overcharged for basic servicing.

Word spread through a college campus first, then a delivery fleet, then sport-bike riders who'd been turned away by workshops without the diagnostic tools for fuel-injected engines. A decade later, the same principle holds: diagnose properly, use the right parts, and explain the invoice in plain language.
