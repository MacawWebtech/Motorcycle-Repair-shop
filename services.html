Every Service Your Motorcycle Will Ever Need.

Eight core services, each backed by a written estimate, genuine parts and a technician who explains what was actually wrong.

Routine Motorcycle Servicing

A scheduled, manufacturer-aligned service that keeps small issues from becoming expensive ones. Every visit includes a multi-point inspection covering fluids, filters, brakes, drivetrain and electricals.

  • Extends engine and drivetrain life
  • Maintains manufacturer warranty compliance
  • Catches wear items before they fail on the road

Oil & Filter Change

Grade-matched synthetic or semi-synthetic oil paired with an OEM or equivalent filter, drained and refilled to the correct level and torque spec.

  • Reduces engine wear and heat build-up
  • Improves fuel efficiency and smoothness
  • Old oil disposed of responsibly

Every 3,000 – 5,000 kmRecommended For

Tyre Inspection & Replacement

Tread depth, sidewall condition and pressure are checked on every visit. Replacements are balanced and fitted to the correct torque with a post-fit alignment check.

  • Improved grip in wet and dry conditions
  • Balanced fitment reduces vibration
  • Compound options for commuter, sport and touring use

Commuter, sport, cruiser, ADVRecommended For

Brake Servicing

Pad thickness, disc wear, caliper function and fluid condition are all inspected. Fluid is bled and replaced on the recommended interval to keep lever feel consistent.

  • Shorter, more predictable stopping distances
  • Prevents caliper seizure and uneven pad wear
  • Fresh fluid protects against brake fade
45 – 60 minutesEstimated Time
Every 10,000 km or 12 monthsRecommended For
Book This Service

Chain Adjustment & Lubrication

Slack is measured against manufacturer spec, sprockets are checked for hooking or wear, and the chain is cleaned and lubricated for quieter, smoother power delivery.

  • Reduces drivetrain snatch and noise
  • Prevents premature sprocket wear
  • Improves power delivery efficiency
20 – 30 minutesEstimated Time
Chain-driven motorcyclesRecommended For

Battery Inspection & Replacement

Load testing reveals a weak battery before it leaves you stranded. Terminals are cleaned and torqued, and replacements are matched to the correct CCA rating.

  • Reliable cold starts
  • Prevents charging system strain
  • Correct disposal of old batteries
