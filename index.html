About The Workshop

Built for Bikes. Trusted by Riders.

Torque & Tread started in a two-bay garage in 2015, fixing commuter bikes for the neighborhood. A decade on, the same obsession with getting the small things right — correct torque values, clean wiring, honest advice — now serves everything from delivery workhorses to liter-class sport bikes.
