Engine Diagnostics

ECU fault scanning, compression testing and live data analysis to find the real cause of a performance issue.

What's Included

A full engine diagnostic starts with an ECU fault code scan, followed by live sensor data readouts while the engine runs through its rev range. Where fuelling or ignition issues are suspected, we follow up with a compression test on each cylinder to rule out mechanical wear.

Every diagnostic concludes with a written report explaining exactly what was found, in plain language, along with a recommended repair plan and cost estimate before any further work begins.

Service Benefits

  • Pinpoints intermittent faults that a road test alone would miss
  • Avoids unnecessary parts replacement by confirming the actual cause
  • Detailed printed diagnostic report for your records
  • Recommended by manufacturers after any major fault light
